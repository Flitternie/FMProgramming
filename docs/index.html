Resource-efficient Foundation Model Programs

Program-guided multimodal reasoning that learns to route tasks across foundation model backends, delivering up to 98% cost savings with minimal accuracy loss on streaming VQA benchmarks.

Workflow at a Glance

  • Program synthesis captures task structure
  • Gradient Thompson Sampling adapts to inputs
  • Structured REINFORCE allocates resources
  • Lightweight detectors screen easy cases
  • High-capacity MLLMs reserved for hard queries
Offline synthesis produces modular programs with conditional control flow tailored to each query.
Online policy observes intermediate signals and switches between backends with differing latency and accuracy.
Feedback after each streamed example continuously refines routing for future inputs.

Streaming Benchmarks

Streaming Binary VQA: 33 compositional queries >2K images each, testing spatial, logical, and numerical reasoning over sequences.
Streaming Open-form VQA: 50 diverse prompts with diffusion-generated imagery, including unanswerable distractors to probe robustness.

Cost-accuracy Frontiers

Key Takeaways

FMPs provide a modality-agnostic recipe for composing heterogeneous models into executable reasoning programs.
Structured REINFORCE with Thompson sampling offers no-regret online learning for per-module backend routing.
Empirical results show consistent Pareto dominance across benchmarks, even with constrained backend pools.
